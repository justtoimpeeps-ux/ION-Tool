# Equipment supply and aerial cargo — working source

This continues recovery/v4-2-reconstruction after f3b9945. The requested 2fd27d9 exists in history; main/origin main remains 718ae90c0a731e785474c3f5baba88748baff112. No new edition, dist rebuild, package version or release tag.

Equipment / fleet is beside the configuration/date controls. Select an equipment model or vehicle, record a documented existing-stock receipt or place an order. Internal Spartan/VC orders debit actual producer warehouse/campaign materials once and reserve staffed fabrication cells. Commercial orders record a separate equipment purchase transaction. Delivery creates finite spare inventory, never additional operating production. Scheduling requires stock or allocated future receipts; completion consumes the reservation once and recovers removed/replaced hardware. Source inventories and deployed reference ground fleets are preserved as adopted opening ownership. No spare machines or drones are invented. Operating fleet settings are capped by deployed ownership; ground vehicles also need handlers.

Mining, mineral processing and raw-material preparation models are Spartan, retaining save-compatible model IDs, speeds and capabilities. Internal quotes have no external equipment purchase cost; material bills and fabrication hours remain explicit adopted estimates. Add/purchase miners through the machine catalogue, place them in Proposal, then schedule. Building orders are calendar capital planning, not a full routed per-part manufacturing recipe simulation. Skipping dates can complete reserved capital jobs/delivery; it does not post operating-day production or wages. Internal labor obligations are exported separately, avoiding duplicate wages. A producer cell with a fabrication booking is conservatively reserved for the entire operating day, with normal recipe/WIP paused. No double use of the same capacity. Reconfiguring a booked fabrication cell is blocked until its jobs finish.

The sidebar uses a physical before/after installation plan: four parallel crews by default, editable crew and path-work rates, roughly quarter-day small jobs to two-day heavy cells. Supplier lead and delivery readiness are separate from crew work; a batch no longer adds every machine's supplier quote consecutively. Minimum schedule duration enforces readiness and parallel work. One pending whole-configuration implementation project remains supported; jobs within that project run in parallel. Separately scheduled concurrent projects are not implemented by this batch.

Spartan cargo drones start at zero. Receive/order them, deploy owned stock, then choose their active count in Operating day settings. Each has finite mass/volume/envelope cargo limits, exclusive availability, load/unload time, energy and actual inventory pickup/drop events. Direct same-site flights ignore ground obstacles and each other's collisions; cross-building reposition flights remain timed. Underground inter-level transfers retain Deep Vein's shaft rather than bypass it. Drones have no fictional paid drivers. Animated repulsor pods and bobbing bodies carry upright large material icons, with distinct campus symbols. Ground cart/walk behavior remains unchanged with zero drones. Cart paths are almost black; walk paths dark gray, retaining road markings.

Validation covers stock, orders, unique transaction IDs, manufacturer allocations, fleet ownership and reservations. Equipment and transactions export in the reconciliation report and workspace. Existing legacy projects/replays remain compatible; legacy pending projects are grandfathered without new equipment obligations. The complete physical regression projection omits only the added zero-valued drones config key, alongside the existing authorized payroll normalization.

Verification: 115 core tests, including finite factory materials/capacity, duplicate prevention, held delivered stock, stock-consuming installation/recovery, failed-order atomicity, parallel durations, no-driver/no-road drone flights, cargo conservation, replay validation/overweight/overlap rejection, and normal-work exclusion during capital fabrication. Browser checks cover existing 15-site navigation/Deep Vein payroll/acceptance/save/reload, equipment inventory/deployment/ownership cap/factory order/export validation, and delete/Undo. Additional visual check status is recorded in the active handoff.

Performance: drones use bounded direct waypoints, not ground routefinding or collision reservations. More active drones still produce more trips/replay records. Default zero drones preserves complete historical physical results in multi-station, Deep Vein and Spartan HQ comparisons. No claim of a measured general operating-day speedup from this feature batch.
