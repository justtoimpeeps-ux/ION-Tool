# V4 day model and remaining limits

The initial replay design is now implemented as a deterministic day calculator plus recorded playback. A separately tracked pre-shift phase stages missing material and services machines with real available staff/parts, followed by nine production hours by default. Pickups/drops change finite stock; complete recipes start timed model batches; unfinished cycles and all buffers carry to the next accepted day. Replay/seeking cannot post a day twice.

The register does not locate its approximately 750 engines of WIP. A new ledger therefore exposes an assumed stage allocation: intermediates at consuming stations and surplus at Receiving, capped by the configured opening engine-equivalent mass budget. Set zero to avoid this assumption. Internal-only opening work can be phased across estimated remaining cycle times; this is an editable initialization assumption, not a measured progress census. Subsequent days start from recorded balances rather than restocking for free. Incoming supply amounts are modeled receipts, not external shipping orders.

Physical loading limits use estimated mass, volume and item envelope. Fleet/driver identities persist across building/campus scenes. Directed belts are actual deliveries. Carrier cell/time reservations cover trailing length and disallow head-on edge swaps. A vehicle yields in a holding area before departure until its whole route is clear. The scheduler finds the earliest clear complete-trip window, uses a rolling dispatch lead time, and clears downstream output before upstream refills. It can still delay a load more than a dispatcher with optimized intermediate waiting and rerouting would. A machine may start its next cycle while a finished item awaits an already scheduled pickup, only when the pickup will free space before that cycle completes. It does not claim optimal traffic or exact industrial turning geometry.

Role clothing and tier-colored hats distinguish workers; people arrive and work at stations, technicians walk/service during preparation, and carriers show cargo. Operator/technician walks do not yet participate in a full pedestrian collision model. Maintenance consumes service kits and staff attendance, restores a configured condition target above a threshold, and productive hours cause configurable wear. Random failures are unspecified.

The user's later decision supersedes the original progression idea: **skills remain fixed; worker-to-tile assignments are preserved, with no automatic experience or promotions.** Daily accounting separates paid and productive hours by process and role. The existing hired roster is paid during the normal shift by default; early attendance adds cost. Unmodeled duties, utilities, outside freight and material purchasing are not falsely included as known accounting figures.

Implementation schedules keep old production reports in force before completion and switch on the completion date. Both configurations, dated reports, equipment inventories, estimates, recorded spending and operating ledgers travel in complete workspace and document-handoff exports. Calendar advancement applies due changes but does not generate invented production for skipped days. Multi-site creation provides empty layouts; unknown mine/refinery recipes and interstellar logistics remain undefined.
