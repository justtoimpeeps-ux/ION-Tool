# IE-4 reference-aligned reconstruction

The user resupplied the PORT ENGINE cutaway during reconstruction. Its labels show ion acceleration grids, vectoring grids, ionization chamber, magnet ring, control charge inputs, particle energizer, injector-relay housing, propellant pump and solar power lines. The planner's IE-4 name remains a project designation; the drawing does not establish exact canonical IE-4 dimensions, rates or material specifications.

The eight retained material IDs now present injector-relay housing/frame (`housing`), magnet ring (`field`), grid/energizer cassette (`energy`), propellant pump/injection (`gas`), charge-input control electronics (`electronic`), ionization chamber/thermal jacket (`thermal`), power-feed/mounting interfaces (`interface`), and sensors/safety (`sensor`). Quantities and dependency identities remain unchanged. `ION_IE4_Raw_Recipe_Unchanged.csv` is generated only after exact comparison with recovered V4.1's expanded recipe.

The engine fronts left: grids and chamber, magnet ring, larger rectangular injector housing, paired blue pump tubes and curved gold power lines. A reference-derived original sprite sheet was generated using the supplied cutaway as visual guidance. Its unmodified generated PNG is retained at `assets/source/reference-engine-sheet.png`. It contains the eight groups and assembled/cutaway/certified/packaged engine states. It reconstructs artwork; it is not recovered V4.2 artwork or a reproduction of the source document.

`generate_v4_2_art.py` retains the recovered machinery designs and creates coordinated source geometry. `render_atlases.cjs` composes that machinery with reference-derived workpieces, then renders WebP atlases, palette images and four work frames per process. Palette images, stored parts, finished output awaiting pickup, cargo and machine workpieces share this artwork. `sprite-assets.js` contains crop coordinates and aspect-preserving drawing; `process-motion.js` uses the same fitted coordinate system. Work motion depends on recorded work state and freezes on pause. The standalone package embeds every atlas, palette image and worker source.

The design catalogue contains adopted industrial equivalents and planning fabrication bills, not sourced vendor quotations. VC generation I has no external equipment purchase price, a warehouse material bill, explicit fabrication man-hour obligation, longer minimum lead time and 6% higher nominal throughput than the strongest commercial option. The bill is allocated once against implemented warehouse balances at scheduling. Completion marks that allocation installed without another debit. Actual spending is recorded separately. Fabrication man-hours are planned obligations, not invented paid attendance.

## Detailed machinery and idle state

Three additional generated sheets (`machinery-upstream.png`, `machinery-parts.png`, `machinery-final.png`) cover the retained 33 process roles. They use the engine reference's restrained metallic/blue/copper visual family, with process-specific machinery, tooling and central work spaces. The renderer composes the same reference-derived component/engine artwork into the loaded plate and palette preview. Idle plates retain an empty fixture and lower intensity. Raster entries are combined with lightweight canvas mechanism motion; this is not a claim that each atlas plate is a separate fully rendered 3D animation frame. Actual buffered output, reserved WIP and recorded work determine what appears during replay.
