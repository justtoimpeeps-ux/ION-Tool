Factory-editor integration
Each internal recipe becomes an operation node attached to compatible owned machines or proposed new workstations. Nodes track units/batch, input quantities, materials, cycle times when known, queue buffers, WIP, reject/rework, machine availability and output routing. Unknown per-stage cycle times, component allowances and purchase lead times remain unspecified until measured or approved; the editor must not invent them. Logistics handles live orders and transport; this register governs the recipes and capability list.
