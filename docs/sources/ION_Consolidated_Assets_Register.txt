16B | Plain-English accounting and operations glossary
Term | Plain-English meaning
Operating profit | Annual operating sales less operating costs, before interest and income taxes.
Revenue | Sales to customers; internal ION division transfers are removed from group revenue.
Assets | Property, cash, inventory, receivables and other resources owned or controlled.
Liabilities | Debts and amounts owed to suppliers, staff and customers.
Book equity | Assets minus liabilities; not cash or acquisition value.
Work in process | Partially made assemblies and engines; not finished inventory.
Throughput | Actual quantity passing through a step over a defined time.
Sustainable capacity | Repeatable output without extraordinary overtime or deferred maintenance.
Nameplate capacity | Maximum designed rate, not a guaranteed achievable production rate.
